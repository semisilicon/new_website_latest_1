Our Services

RTL Design

End-to-end RTL development for ASIC and FPGA platforms using Verilog/SystemVerilog. Expertise in micro-architecture design, synthesis-friendly coding, timing closure, and custom IP.

Verification

UVM-based verification for complex ASIC/SoC designs — scalable testbenches, constrained-random verification, assertions (SVA), and automation for regression and coverage closure.

Firmware Validation

Board bring-up, silicon debugging and system-level validation using JTAG, oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, and lab tools. Firmware bring-up, stress testing and platform reliability analysis.

Post-Silicon Test & Characterization

Functional characterization, performance tuning and silicon validation across environmental and electrical corners. Custom test development for production validation.

Automation & Test Infrastructure

Test automation frameworks, continuous integration for silicon/firmware regression, bench orchestration and data collection for analytics.

Consulting & IP Reuse

Architecture reviews, RTL/code quality audits, and IP integration services to accelerate time-to-market while minimizing silicon risk.
